Built for owners who wear too many hats

Most businesses don't fail from a bad idea — they stall because the owner is still the bottleneck for every decision. Certified PAI is an advisory practice for owners who want their company to run on a system, not on their memory.

We work directly with your leadership team: naming who owns what, agreeing on the handful of numbers that actually predict trouble, and giving you a meeting rhythm that ends with decisions instead of another meeting.

What we do together

Each engagement follows the same arc — diagnose, install, run — adapted to where your business actually is.

Diagnose

A structured look at where the business is losing time and money: unclear ownership, meetings that don't decide anything, numbers nobody trusts.

Install

We build the accountability chart, the scorecard, and the quarterly planning rhythm with your leadership team — not for them.

Run

Ongoing quarterly and annual sessions to keep the system honest as the business grows, plus the EOS Dashboard to track it week to week.

Coach the owner

One-on-one sessions focused on the decisions only you can make, and getting everything else off your desk.
